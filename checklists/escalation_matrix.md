# Escalation Matrix

- Ask Legal: matter status, legal constraints, disclosure requirements, contract terms.
- Ask Finance: accruals, reserves, cash flow, covenant calculations, forecast impact.
- Ask Auditor: evidence, audit scope, ICFR, going concern, recognition and measurement.
- Ask IR: consistency with public messaging, earnings scripts, investor materials.
- Ask Management / Board: governance, resources, remediation, disclosure committee review.
