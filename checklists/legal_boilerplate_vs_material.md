# Legal Boilerplate Vs Material Checklist

- Does the paragraph only use hypothetical terms such as may, could, or from time to time?
- Does it name a regulator, court, counterparty, agreement, or proceeding?
- Does it mention an amount, accrual, reserve, covenant, waiver, subpoena, notice, or material weakness?
- Did wording change from the prior year?
- Does the paragraph connect to MD&A, footnotes, liquidity, controls, or governance?
