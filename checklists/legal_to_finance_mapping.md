# Legal-To-Finance Mapping Checklist

- Legal proceedings: contingency footnote, accruals, insurance recoveries, subsequent events.
- Internal control: auditor opinion, remediation cost, restatement risk, close process.
- Debt covenants: liquidity, classification of debt, waivers, going concern.
- Related parties: balances, pricing, approvals, collectability, governance.
- Guarantees and commitments: maximum exposure, timing, off-balance-sheet disclosure.
- Dilution: diluted EPS, warrants, convertibles, share count, financing flexibility.
