# Risk Taxonomy

## `audit_going_concern`

Covers substantial doubt and going-concern language. Finance readers should
care about liquidity, cash runway, audit emphasis, refinancing, and disclosure.
Signals include `going concern`, `substantial doubt`, and `ability to continue`.
Owners: Finance, Auditor, Legal, Board.

## `internal_control_reporting`

Covers ICFR, SOX, material weaknesses, disclosure controls, remediation, late
filing, and restatement. Finance readers should care about close quality,
audit risk, reporting reliability, and remediation timing. Owners: Auditor,
Finance, Legal.

## `legal_proceedings_litigation`

Covers litigation, subpoenas, investigations, settlements, arbitration, and
contingencies. Finance readers should care about accrual, range of loss,
insurance, timing, and disclosure wording. Owners: Legal, Finance, IR.

## `regulatory_trade_policy`

Covers tariffs, customs, sanctions, export controls, forced-labor restrictions,
trade policy, and tax-credit eligibility. Finance readers should care about
cost, supply chain, revenue eligibility, and compliance exposure. Owners:
Legal, Finance, Management.

## `related_party_governance`

Covers related-party transactions, affiliates, common control, controlling
shareholders, VIEs, and governance conflicts. Finance readers should care about
revenue quality, receivables, fairness, control, and approval processes. Owners:
Legal, Auditor, Finance, Board.

## `debt_liquidity_covenant`

Covers debt covenants, defaults, waivers, credit facilities, working-capital
deficits, refinancing, and liquidity. Finance readers should care about debt
classification, default risk, cash runway, and going-concern linkage. Owners:
Finance, Legal, Auditor.

## `guarantees_commitments`

Covers guarantees, purchase obligations, capacity commitments, indemnities,
factory ramp, customer concentration, and supplier concentration. Finance
readers should care about off-balance-sheet exposure, liquidity, gross margin,
capex, and concentration risk. Owners: Finance, Legal, Management.

## `equity_dilution_control`

Covers warrants, convertible notes, earnouts, sponsor arrangements,
registration rights, redemption history, lock-ups, voting rights, and dilution.
Finance readers should care about EPS, ownership, fair value, control, and
liquidity. Owners: Finance, Legal, IR.

## `tax_cross_border`

Covers uncertain tax, transfer pricing, withholding, cross-border tax, tax
audits, offshore holding, remittance restrictions, and valuation allowance.
Finance readers should care about effective tax rate, cash tax, deferred tax,
and jurisdictional exposure. Owners: Finance, Legal, Auditor.

## `management_board_governance`

Covers board oversight, audit committee matters, director independence,
officer transitions, governance exemptions, and home-country practices. Finance
readers should care about oversight quality, disclosure process, and investor
confidence. Owners: Board, Legal, IR.

## `disclosure_ir_consistency`

Covers safe harbor, forward-looking statements, guidance, non-GAAP language,
and risk-factor consistency. Finance readers should care about whether external
messaging overstates certainty. Owners: Legal, IR, Finance.

## `cybersecurity_governance`

Covers cybersecurity governance, data breach, ransomware, incident response,
vendor risk, materiality assessment, and board reporting. Finance readers
should care about incident cost, disclosure timing, controls, and operational
resilience. Owners: Legal, Management, Board.

## `material_contracts`

Covers material agreements, supply agreements, licenses, customer or supplier
concentration, termination rights, exclusivity, backlog, and commitments.
Finance readers should care about revenue durability, margin, concentration,
capex, and termination risk. Owners: Legal, Finance.
