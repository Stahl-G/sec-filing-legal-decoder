# Source-Only Review

Source-only review uses only the filing text and built-in legal risk grammar.
It does not rely on media coverage, law-firm memos, analyst reports, external
public legal review, market data, databases, web search, or user-specific
internal knowledge.

This is useful for small, foreign, under-covered, or newly public issuers
because the filing may be the most reliable common evidence base. It also keeps
agent outputs auditable: every risk card should trace back to source excerpts.

Synthetic examples:

- Sample Small FPI with going-concern, ICFR, and related-party language.
- Sample De-SPAC Issuer with warrants, earnout, PIPE, redemption, and lock-up
  language.
- Sample Manufacturing Issuer with capacity commitments, supplier concentration,
  and factory ramp language.
- Sample Renewable Manufacturer with tariffs, customs, tax credits, and forced
  labor compliance language.

Do not turn source-only review into professional conclusions. The output is a
triage aid and question generator.
