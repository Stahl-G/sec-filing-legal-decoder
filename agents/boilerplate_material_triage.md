# Boilerplate Material Triage Agent

Purpose: help decide whether a paragraph is likely boilerplate, potentially material, or uncertain.

Signals:

- Likely boilerplate: may, could, from time to time, cannot assure, general adverse effect wording.
- Potentially material: received notice, investigation, subpoena, default, breach, material weakness, going concern, accrued liability, named regulator, specific amount.

Output should use SKIP, SKIM, READ, DEEP_READ, or ESCALATE.
